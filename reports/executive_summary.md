## 1. Module A: Retail Revenue Intelligence — Quantified Findings

### Finding 1: Furniture Margin Collapse from Uncontrolled Discounting
- **Calculated Metric**: Furniture generated **$741,999.80 in Sales (32.30% of enterprise revenue)** but produced only **$18,451.27 in Total Profit (6.44% of total profit)**, delivering a fragile **2.49% profit margin**.
- **Comparison**: In contrast, Technology delivered **$836,154.03 in Sales** and **$145,454.95 in Profit (17.40% margin)**, while Office Supplies delivered **$719,047.03 in Sales** and **$122,490.80 in Profit (17.04% margin)**.
- **Root Cause**: Tables and Bookcases sub-categories suffered from aggressive discounting (>0.20), destroying operating profit.

### Finding 2: Sub-Category Loss Leaders ($17.7k Net Loss in Tables)
- **Calculated Metric**: Three product sub-categories produced cumulative negative profits totaling **-$22,476.51**:
  - **Tables**: -$17,725.48 net loss on $206,965.53 sales (-8.56% margin).
  - **Bookcases**: -$3,472.56 net loss on $114,879.99 sales (-3.02% margin).
  - **Supplies**: -$1,278.47 net loss on $46,673.53 sales (-2.74% margin).
- **Executive Takeaway**: Tables represent the single largest commercial drain in the enterprise catalog.

### Finding 3: Severe Profit Destruction Beyond the 20% Discount Threshold
- **Calculated Metric**:
  - Orders with **0% discount** yielded a healthy **29.93% profit margin** ($1,077,183 sales, $322,414 profit).
  - Orders with **1% to 15% discount** yielded an acceptable **13.79% profit margin** ($388,421 sales, $53,576 profit).
  - Orders discounted **greater than 20%** produced net operating losses:
    - 21% - 50% discount: **-$34,818 profit** on $447,215 sales (-7.79% margin).
    - >50% discount: **-$54,775 profit** on $148,874 sales (-36.79% margin).
- **Commercial Total**: 1,871 loss-making transactions destroyed **-$156,131.29** in gross profits.

### Finding 4: Regional Performance Divergence (West Leadership vs. Central Losses)
- **Calculated Metric**:
  - **West Region**: #1 in sales ($725,457.82) and #1 in profit ($108,418.45) with a leading **14.94% margin**.
  - **East Region**: $678,781.24 sales, $91,522.78 profit (**13.48% margin**).
  - **Central Region**: $501,239.89 sales, but generated only $39,706.36 profit (**7.92% margin**), burdened by high average discount rates (17.2%) in Texas and Illinois.

### Finding 5: Pronounced Q4 Holiday Seasonality and Sales Acceleration
- **Calculated Metric**:
  - Average sales in Q4 ($791,245 across 4 years) outpaced Q1 ($359,483) by **120.1%**.
  - Monthly peak occurs consistently in **November ($352,461 across 4 years)** and **December ($325,294)**.
  - 2018 full-year revenue reached **$733,215**, reflecting a **+20.30% YoY growth** over 2017 ($609,205).

## 2. Module C: Customer Retention Intelligence — Findings & Recommendations

### Verified Findings:
1. **Baseline Churn Rate**: **26.54%** (1,869 churned out of 7,043 eligible accounts).
2. **Monthly Revenue Exposure**: **$139,130.85 per month** is lost to churned subscribers, representing **30.50% of monthly recurring revenue**.
3. **Tenure Cohort Cliff**: The first 12 months represent the critical risk window, exhibiting a **47.44% churn rate** (1,037 churned out of 2,186 subscribers). In contrast, accounts past 60 months tenure exhibit an exceptional **93.39% retention rate** (6.61% churn).
4. **Contract Vulnerability**: Month-to-month contracts suffer a **42.71% churn rate**, compared to **11.27%** for one-year contracts and **2.83%** for two-year contracts.
5. **Support Service Decoupling**: Fiber optic subscribers without Tech Support churned at an alarming **41.6%**, whereas fiber optic subscribers with Tech Support and Online Security churned at only **15.4%**.

### Prioritized Actionable Recommendations:
1. **Tier 1 (Immediate Onboarding Safeguard)**: Establish a dedicated 90-day onboarding milestone protocol with proactive usage check-ins and free 3-month trials of Tech Support and Online Security for new subscribers.
2. **Tier 2 (Contract Migration Incentive)**: Deploy targeted $5-$10/month contract renewal discounts or device subsidies to migrate Month-to-month subscribers into 12-month commitments before Month 6.
3. **Tier 3 (Payment Modernization)**: Incentivize migration away from Electronic Check (45.3% churn) toward automated credit card or bank transfer autopay with a one-time $10 statement credit.
